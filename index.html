Grad Job Search Engine | Improving Job Search Accuracy | Research + Machine Learning Prototype

Many “entry-level” jobs require years of experience, which makes it confusing for students coming out of college to know what they actually qualify for.

I looked at how job listings are labeled, where those labels do not match the real requirements, and how the information could be structured in a way that feels more honest and easier to understand.

  • Manually labeled 1,000 job postings to identify patterns
  • Analyzed a dataset of 500,000 listings
  • Defined criteria for misleading job classifications
  • Built filtering logic based on those criteria
  • Explored BERT for natural language classification
  • Tested results with users to evaluate clarity and trust
